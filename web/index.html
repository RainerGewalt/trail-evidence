            What a passing result means
            
      
            
        Every content_hash and chain_hash was recomputed from the
        record itself (RFC 8785 canonical JSON, SHA-256) and matched, every record links to
        the declared chain_hash of its predecessor, and sequence numbers increase
        by one per stream. No record in this file was altered, removed or reordered relative to
        the others.
      
            
      
            
        It does not say that the payloads are true, that the clocks were right, or that nobody
        rewrote the whole file. Signatures bind the chain to a key; give the public key above to
        have them checked. Anchoring the latest chain_hash elsewhere binds it to a
        point in time. The specification lists these limits in section 6.3.
      
            
      
            
        Every record gets one of four states. valid: all checks ran and passed.
        invalid: a check failed (schema_invalid, hash_mismatch,
        chain_break, seq_mismatch, signature_invalid,
        profile_invalid). unknown: the record's own hashes are correct, but its
        predecessor is not in the file, so its link cannot be checked (seq_gap); a record that
        links correctly to it is valid relative to it (after_unknown).
        outside scope: the record names another format or major version and is not checked.
      
            
      
            
        A journal passes when no record is invalid. Unknown and out-of-scope records are counted, not failed:
        deleting a record leaves its successor unknown rather than invalid. Tick Strict to fail on them,
        and on profiles this page does not know. A record's profile state (none, valid,
        invalid, outside_scope) says whether its payload was checked against the
        schema of its profile.
