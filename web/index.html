        What a passing result means
        
      
        
        Every content_hash and chain_hash was recomputed from the
        record itself (RFC 8785 canonical JSON, SHA-256) and matched, every record links to
        the declared chain_hash of its predecessor, and sequence numbers increase
        by one per stream. No record in this file was altered, removed or reordered relative to
        the others.
      
        
      
        
        It does not say that the payloads are true, that the clocks were right, or that nobody
        rewrote the whole file. Signatures bind the chain to a key; give the public key above to
        have them checked. Anchoring the latest chain_hash elsewhere binds it to a
        point in time. The specification lists these limits in section 6.3.
      
        
      
        
        Classes: schema_invalid, hash_mismatch, chain_break,
        seq_gap and signature_invalid are errors and fail the journal.
        restart and number_precision are notices.
